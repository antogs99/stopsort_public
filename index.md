# Stopsort Support

Stopsort puts your stops in the fastest order. Add addresses in any order, tap Sort, then Navigate hands the route to Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze. iPhone only.

**Need help, found a bug, or have an idea?** Email **antogs99@gmail.com**. We read every message.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my purchase?** Open Settings in Stopsort and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

**Why does Navigate open only some of my stops?** Apple Maps takes up to 15 stops in one go and Google Maps up to 10. Waze takes one stop at a time. When you finish that part of the route, tap Navigate again for the rest.

**How many stops can a route have?** Free routes hold up to 12 stops. Stopsort Pro sorts up to 200.

**What are folders for?** Keep routes together by client, day or job. Free has one folder with up to two routes; Pro has no limit. Deleting a folder keeps its routes.

**Can I paste a list of addresses?** Yes, with Pro. Put one address per line (or separate them with semicolons). Numbering like "1." or "-" is ignored. Include the city and state for best results.

**Where are my routes stored?** On your iPhone only. There is no account and we never receive your addresses.
