# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026
**App:** Stopsort
**Developer:** antogs99
**Contact:** antogs99@gmail.com

## 1. Summary

Stopsort is a multi-stop route planner. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours that stores your routes. Your routes, stops, folders, notes and settings stay on your iPhone, and the stop order is worked out on the device. We never receive your addresses or your location. The only data we collect is anonymous usage analytics, described in section 6.

## 2. Information You Create

Routes, stops, addresses, notes, done marks and folders are stored locally on your iPhone. We do not receive this information, and Stopsort does not upload or sync it anywhere.

## 3. Location

If you allow it, Stopsort uses your current location as the start of a route and to show you on the map, only while you are using the app. Your location is not stored by us, sent to us or used for analytics. You can change this permission in iOS Settings at any time; you can still choose a start address by hand.

## 4. Apple Maps

To find addresses you type or paste, draw the map and work out driving times between stops, Stopsort sends the address text and stop coordinates to Apple Maps. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 5. Navigation and Sharing

When you tap Navigate, Stopsort opens Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze, whichever you choose, with the coordinates of the stops being navigated. From then on that app handles them under its own privacy terms. When you share a route, Stopsort creates text and a CSV file and passes them to the app or person you choose. We never receive them.

## 6. Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It collects events such as screens viewed, features used (for example, sorting a route, tapping Navigate or opening the paywall), counts and timings (such as the number of stops in a route or how long sorting took), your answer to "what do you use Stopsort for" during onboarding, your chosen maps app, purchase outcomes and amounts, app version, device model, operating system version, approximate region, and a random app-instance identifier generated for the app installation.

- This data is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. We never collect your addresses, stop names, notes, location or route details.
- It is used for analytics only and is not used for advertising or for tracking across other companies' apps and websites.
- Google processes this data under its own privacy terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third parties involved are Apple (App Store, purchases and Apple Maps), Google (analytics, and Google Maps if you choose it for navigation) and Waze (if you choose it for navigation).

## 8. Children's Privacy

Stopsort is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Routes, folders and settings are stored on your iPhone and are deleted when you delete the app. Files you shared are kept wherever you sent them. Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics according to the retention settings we choose, which are limited in time. Because analytics data is not linked to your identity, we cannot look up or delete data for a specific person. Deleting the app stops further collection from that installation.

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. Because Stopsort does not collect personal information that identifies you, there is generally nothing for us to access or delete, but you can contact us at any time with questions or requests.

## 11. Security

Your routes are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone and are protected by iOS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
