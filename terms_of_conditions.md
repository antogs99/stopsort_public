## 1. About the App

Stopsort is a multi-stop route planner for iPhone. It works out a fast order for the stops you enter and hands the route to a maps app for turn-by-turn directions. By downloading or using it, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

## 2. Drive Safely

Do not enter stops, edit routes or read the screen while driving. Set up your route before you leave, and pull over somewhere safe to make changes. Always follow traffic laws, road signs and the instructions of local authorities. Stopsort does not provide turn-by-turn directions; the maps app you choose does.

## 3. Route Order and Times

Stopsort aims to find the fastest order for your stops, using straight-line estimates and driving times from Apple Maps. The order and the times shown are estimates. They do not account for live traffic, road closures, delivery windows, vehicle restrictions or opening hours, and may not be the fastest possible route. Check that a route makes sense before you rely on it. Addresses are looked up by Apple Maps and may occasionally resolve to the wrong place; check each stop on the map.
